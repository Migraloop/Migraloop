# Local Sync Lab with feature-time Scenario coverage (not CI)

Operators need a real, disposable stack to manually verify Sync through Delivery without assembling Oracle, LogMiner prerequisites, MongoDB, Platform Store, and the app by hand. We introduce **Local Sync Lab**: Lab-provisioned engines and app on the real product path (no mocks/stubs), live DB access for inspect/mutate, and a broad **selectable** catalog of **Lab Scenarios** (self-contained in a **Scenario Namespace**, one Scenario run at a time, recipe-authored intra-Scenario concurrency, correctness and operational metrics with equal weight). A first-class capability is incomplete until Lab Scenario coverage is designed while building it; the Lab catalog is complete only when shipped capabilities are covered. This is manual verification and a feature-completeness bar—not the Release Quality Gate and not “run every Scenario in CI” (see ADR-0011).
